# 🎨 Artie — Digital Art Marketplace

Artie is a modern **Digital Art Marketplace UI/UX Design** where users can discover, explore, and purchase unique digital artworks from independent artists.

## ✨ Features

* Explore digital artwork
* Browse categories and trending art
* View detailed artwork information
* Discover artists
* Add artwork to wishlist
* Purchase digital artwork
* Simple checkout experience
* Responsive desktop design

## 🛠️ Designed With

* **Figma**
* UI/UX Design
* Wireframing

## 🎯 Core Pages

1. **Home**
2. **Artwork Details**
3. **Cart**
